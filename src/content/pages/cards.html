What a card order looks like

A single card is the usual place to start. Sets — a stack of thank-yous, a small holiday batch, place-card cousins — are welcome when you have a count. Market cards are the ready, sleeved pieces. Anything with a name or a private joke is made to order.

Pricing follows the piece. A greeting card from the market table is a small purchase. A custom set is quoted after the wording, the quantity, and the date are clear.
